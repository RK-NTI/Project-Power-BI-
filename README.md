# Project (Power-BI)
# 📊Super Store Sales Analysis 
Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze Super Store sales performance.
A comprehensive, interactive three-tab business intelligence dashboard built to track, analyze, and optimize retail performance across sales, profitability, and customer demographics.
Implemented slicers and filters for dynamic report interaction.
Used Power Query for data transformation and DAX measures for KPI calculations.
Delivered actionable business insights to support sales performance analysis and decision-making.


# 🔍 What I Analyzed & Learned to Build:
• **Executive KPI Cards:** Designed high-level metrics for Total Sales, Total Profit, Quantity Sold, and Profit Margins to give decision-makers an instant snapshot of health at a single glance.

• **Monthly Sales & Profit Trends (Line Charts):** Tracked performance over time to quickly pinpoint historical seasonality, peak revenue months, and periods requiring promotional boosts.

• **Category & Segment Deep Dives (Donut/Bar Charts):** Compared performance across product categories (Technology, Office Supplies, Furniture) and customer segments to see exactly what drives the most value.

• **Regional & Shipping Breakdowns:** Mapped out geographical performance and shipping modes to highlight operational bottlenecks and top-performing territories.

• **Dynamic Interactive Slicers:** Implemented filters for Year, Region, Sub-Category, and Category, allowing users to cut the data instantly and answer hyper-specific business questions on the fly.
